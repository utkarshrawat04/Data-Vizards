Beyond the
headlines.

A road crash is more than a number in a report.

Our story brings together Kolkata Police fatal and non-fatal crash records from 2021 to 2023, and a separate dataset of bus-stop locations. Explore patterns in recorded incidents — without confusing crash counts with exposure-adjusted risk.

The data includes missing locations and differences in reporting fields. Geographic visualisations show only records with usable coordinates.

Reading the data responsibly

Counts describe recorded cases, not population- or traffic-adjusted risk. Missing coordinates limit spatial analyses. Proximity to a stop does not establish that the stop caused a crash. For field definitions and provenance, consult the project documentation.
